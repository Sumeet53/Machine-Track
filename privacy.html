Privacy Policy for MachineTrack

Effective Date: October 2026

Opal Luna Labs operates the MachineTrack application ("the App"). This page informs users regarding our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data.

Information Collection and Use

MachineTrack is designed as a standalone operational utility for tracking machine status, uptime, and maintenance logs. The App stores machine operational parameters, maintenance entries, and shift data locally on your device (using browser LocalStorage / IndexedDB). We do not collect, transmit, or sell any personal identifiable information (PII) to remote servers.

Permissions

The App does not request access to device location, camera, microphone, or user contacts.

Third-Party Services

The App is hosted via GitHub Pages and packaged using standard Progressive Web App / Trusted Web Activity protocols. We do not integrate third-party tracking or behavioral analytics SDKs that profile individual users.

Children's Privacy

The App is an industrial utility intended for workplace operations and does not address anyone under the age of 18.

Contact Us

If you have questions regarding this Privacy Policy, you may contact Opal Luna Labs via our developer channels.
