MachineTrack — Account and Data Deletion Request

If you have registered an account with MachineTrack (by Opal Luna Labs) and wish to permanently delete your account and all associated cloud data, follow the instructions below.

How to Request Deletion

Send an email to your registered developer email or support contact with the subject line:

"Request Account Deletion — MachineTrack"

Please include the email address you used to create your MachineTrack account.

What Happens When You Request Deletion?

  • Account Deletion: Your user authentication credentials will be permanently removed from Firebase Authentication.
  • Data Deletion: All factory settings, machine profiles, and daily shift/maintenance logs stored under your user profile in Cloud Firestore will be deleted permanently.
  • Timeline: Account deletion requests are typically processed within 7 business days of verification.

Data Retention

No operational logs, machine parameters, or personal email addresses are retained after account deletion is completed.
